# IronFit Movement Mirror — Trainer Guide

*A movement-coaching mirror with a memory.*

IronFit Movement Mirror lets your clients rehearse their FRS movements in front of
a camera and see, in real time, how their range and control compare to a clean
reference you recorded. Every rehearsal rolls up into a dashboard that tells you
who needs your attention and why — so your coaching time goes where it matters.

This guide walks you through everything: setting up your profile, adding clients,
building movements, programming a client's focus, reading the dashboard, and what
your clients experience on their side.

---

## 1. The big picture

There are two kinds of people in the app:

- **Trainers** (you, Lee, Leah). You build movements, coach clients, and share an
  intake link that brings new clients onto your roster.
- **Clients.** They rehearse movements, watch their own progress, and message you.

A client always belongs to **one trainer** — the one whose intake link they used.
All three of you also share a **practice-wide Overview** (see section 8).

> **Privacy first:** a client's camera video never leaves their device. Only the
> movement *scores* (range achieved, any compensations, camera confidence) are
> saved and shared with you. There is no video of your clients stored anywhere.

---

## 2. Getting started — your profile

When you first sign in you'll see a short welcome walkthrough. After that, open the
**Settings** tab (bottom navigation) and fill in:

- **Display name** — what your clients see.
- **Phone** — shown to your clients so they can reach you.
- **Short bio** — optional, a sentence or two about you.
- **Welcome note for new clients** — a personal message shown to each new client
  on their first login. A warm one sentence goes a long way, e.g. *"Glad to have
  you — rehearse a little each day and message me any time."*

Settings is also where you'll find **your intake link** (next section).

---

## 3. Adding clients — your intake link

You don't add clients manually. Instead, you share **your intake link** — a unique
web address tied to your account. You'll find it in **Settings** and on your
welcome screen, with a **Copy link** button.

It looks like:

```
https://your-app-url/intake/LEAH-421B
```

**How it works:**

1. Send your link to a prospective client (text, email, however you like).
2. They open it and fill out a short intake form: name, email, a password,
   phone, their goals, injury/health history, experience level, an emergency
   contact, and a consent checkbox.
3. When they submit, their account is created and they **join your roster
   automatically** — no manual assignment, and they can't land on the wrong
   trainer.
4. You'll have their goals and history waiting for you before the first session.

> Each trainer has their **own** link. A client who uses Leah's link becomes
> Leah's client; one who uses Lee's link becomes Lee's. Share the right one.

---

## 4. Building a movement

Open the **Movements** tab. Building a movement is three steps:

1. **Add** — create the movement: name it, choose its type (CAR, PAILs, RAILs,
   flow), its primary joint and side, the target joints to track, the cues you
   coach, and the compensation patterns to watch for.
2. **Upload** — record one clean reference repetition and upload it. The app
   analyzes it and extracts the model range your clients will be measured
   against. You'll see a quality rating (high / reduced / low) so you know the
   reference is good before you rely on it.
3. **Publish** — set the movement **live**. Live movements appear in your
   clients' library. Drafts stay private to you until you're ready.

You can edit, re-record, or archive a movement at any time.

**Tips for a good reference rep:**

- Film in good, even light with the whole body in frame.
- Use the recommended camera angle for that movement.
- Move at a controlled tempo — this is the standard your clients aim for.

---

## 5. Setting a client's focus ("Your focus")

Clients see your full library, but you can **pin a few movements** as their focus
so they know exactly where to start.

1. Open a client from the **Clients** tab.
2. In the **Your focus** section, choose a movement from *"+ Add a movement to
   focus…"*. (Only your *live* movements are selectable.)
3. Add an optional coaching note per movement — e.g. *"slow on the way down,
   pause at end range."*
4. Remove a movement from focus any time with **Remove**.

On the client's **Train** tab, their focus movements appear pinned at the top,
highlighted, with your note — above the full library.

---

## 6. Reading the Clients dashboard

The **Clients** tab is your home base. Each client shows their last activity and
session count, and clients who **need attention sort to the top**. The app
watches for four coaching signals:

- **Recurring compensation** — the same movement fault keeps showing up across
  recent sessions. The client's detail page tells you which one and gives you the
  cue to coach it.
- **ROM stalled or declining** — a movement's range has flattened or is trending
  down, or is sitting below target. Time to intervene.
- **Gone quiet** — the client hasn't rehearsed in a while. A nudge or a message
  helps.
- **Low camera confidence** — their camera setup (framing, lighting) is
  undermining the feedback quality. A quick note on setup fixes it.

A client with none of these shows **On track**.

### The client detail page

Tap a client to see:

- Their **intake** (goals, injuries, experience, contact).
- **Your focus** for them (and where you set it).
- **What to coach** — the recurring faults, each with a ready-to-use cue.
- **Range of motion by movement** — per-movement trend lines (improving,
  holding, declining), latest vs. best.
- A **Message** button to start a conversation.

---

## 7. Messaging

The **Messages** tab is 1:1 chat between you and a client. Messages are private to
the two of you and arrive in real time. You can also jump straight into a
conversation from a client's detail page with the **Message** button.

---

## 8. The practice-wide Overview

The **Overview** tab is shared by all three trainers (you, Lee, and Leah). It
shows **every trainer's roster** in one place: how many clients each has, how many
need attention, and each client's status.

From here you can:

- See the whole practice at a glance.
- Open any client's detail page.
- **Reassign a client** to a different trainer using the dropdown next to their
  name (for example, when someone moves from Lee to Leah).

> Note: every trainer can see every roster in the Overview. Setting a client's
> *focus* is still done by that client's **own** trainer.

---

## 9. What your clients experience

It helps to know what your clients see, so you can explain it. After they join
through your intake link, they get a personalized welcome (with your note), then
three tabs:

- **Train** — their movement library, with **Your focus** pinned on top. They
  pick a movement and rehearse it in front of the camera, seeing their range and
  any compensations against your reference in real time.
- **Progress** — their range improving over time and which faults keep recurring.
- **Messages** — a direct line to you.

**What to tell a new client:**

- *Rehearse between sessions — short and often beats long and rare.*
- *The feedback is a mirror, not a diagnosis. It shows what your body did so we
  can work on it together.*
- *Set your camera up well: good light, full body in frame, the angle the app
  suggests.*
- *Your video stays on your phone. I only see your scores.*

---

## 10. Privacy & limitations

- IronFit Movement Mirror is a **coaching tool**. It does **not** diagnose injury
  or replace in-person assessment by a qualified practitioner.
- Client camera video is processed **on the client's device** and never uploaded
  or stored. Only movement scores are saved.
- Your reference videos are stored privately and are only visible to you and your
  own clients.

---

## 11. FAQ & troubleshooting

**A client says the feedback seems off.**
Check the camera-confidence signal on their detail page. Low confidence almost
always means framing or lighting — ask them to stand back so their whole body is
in frame, in even light, at the recommended angle.

**A client ended up with the wrong trainer.**
Use the **Overview** tab and reassign them with the dropdown next to their name.

**A new client didn't appear on my roster.**
Make sure they used **your** intake link (each trainer has a different one). If
they created an account another way, you can reassign them from the Overview.

**I recorded a reference but the quality is "low."**
Re-record with better light and a fuller view of the body, at the recommended
angle. A clean reference is what every client is measured against, so it's worth
getting right.

**A movement isn't showing up for my clients.**
Confirm it's set to **live** (not draft or archived) in the Movements tab.
